# QSMS 4.8.3 — Heat Number Search & Global Steel Ledger

- RMTC entry begins with Heat Number search.
- Existing RMTC/part/supplier usage is displayed before entry.
- Rejected RMTC Heat Numbers may be reused for a new Supplier or Part Number.
- Active duplicate Supplier–Part combinations under the same Heat Number are blocked.
- Global planned and inward steel across every RMTC, Supplier and Part under the Heat Number cannot exceed the Heat steel quantity.
- Material Inward availability uses the global Heat balance.

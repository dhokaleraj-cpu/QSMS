# QSMS 4.8.8 — Inspection Pending Queue Correction

- Dimensional and MetLAB pending quantities are calculated from eligible Material Inward records, not only from report rows that already exist.
- A newly accepted Material Inward appears immediately as pending for both inspections.
- Draft, pending and on-hold reports remain in the worklist.
- Only a final Accepted, Accepted Under Reserve or Rejected report closes that inspection queue.
- Inspection Home now shows a combined pending worklist with direct links to Dimensional and MetLAB entry.
- Dimensional Entry and MetLAB Entry show their pending lists at the top.
- Dashboard quick-action counts use the same shared pending logic.
